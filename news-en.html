September 27, 2026 Press release

Mohammad Amin Alsalami (LAC BERLIN) finishes seventh at the Asian Championships

Mohammad Amin Alsalami finished the season with a respectable result at the 20th Asian Athletics Championships in Nagoya, Japan, on September 26, 2026. He reached the triple jump final and recorded a distance of 15.90 m in his third attempt, finishing seventh. China’s Su Wen won with 17.42 m.

Despite his strong placing, Mohammad Alsalami was disappointed: “I wanted to at least match my season’s best of 16.07 m at this important competition, but unfortunately I couldn’t manage it today because of the rain.”

The 2026 German triple jump champion holds both German and Syrian citizenship and competes for Syria. He hopes to contribute to the development of the sport in Syria.

July 24–26, 2026 Press release

HELLERSDORFER ATHLETIK-CLUB BERLIN e.V. succeeds at the German Athletics Championships – Emma Goretzka to compete at the U20 World Championships

After the young athletes competing for the LAC BERLIN training partnership had already won two gold, four silver and one bronze medal at their respective German Championships, the women’s and men’s teams also achieved excellent results.

At the German Athletics Championships in Bochum-Wattenscheid from July 24 to 26, 2026, Mohammad Amin Alsalami (LAC BERLIN) was the favourite in the triple jump. Difficult weather conditions made for an exciting competition. In his final attempt, he jumped 15.67 m, beating the leader, Benedikt von Hardenberg (LAC Quelle Fürth), by one centimetre. It was Mohammad Amin Alsalami’s first title after winning silver the previous year.

The women’s 100 m final was also close. Jolina Ernst (TV Wattenscheid 01) won, but Emma Goretzka (LAC BERLIN) crossed the line at the same time in 11.26 seconds. The photo finish awarded the win to Jolina Ernst. Emma had hoped to win a medal and was surprised to finish ahead of sprint stars Gina Lückenkemper (SCC Berlin) and Rebekka Haase (Sprintteam Wetzlar), who placed third and fourth.

For Emma, this competition was a final tune-up for the U20 World Athletics Championships in Eugene, USA, from August 5 to 9, 2026. She hoped to reach the final in both the 100 m and the 4 × 100 m relay. The competition, especially from athletes from the United States and Jamaica, would be a major challenge.
