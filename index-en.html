PORTRAIT

Mohammad Amin Alsalami

Mohammad Amin Alsalami comes from Syria. Until 2015, he lived in Aleppo and was already a successful long jumper. The war destroyed his life, the life of his family and the lives of millions of people in Syria. He had to abandon his sports studies and fled across the Mediterranean.

In Germany, he was looking for a sports club and found AC-BERLIN. With the support of his coach Lutz Kramer, he was able to continue his sporting development and has become one of Germany's leading long and triple jumpers.

Many friends supported him on his journey. In 2023, he was selected for the IOC Refugee Team. His biggest sporting achievement so far was competing at the 2024 Olympic Games in Paris, where he competed in the long jump.

In 2025, he became a German citizen. In 2026, he won the gold medal in the triple jump at the German Athletics Championships.

Mohammad is now a role model for many people with refugee and migrant backgrounds in Germany.

Not everyone makes it to the Olympic Games. But sport at AC-BERLIN is possible for many people. The club offers sports programs in different departments and creates opportunities for personal and sporting development.
